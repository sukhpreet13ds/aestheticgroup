Meet Our Founder – Mr. Sushant Muttreja

With over 25 years of experience in the real estate industry, Sushant Muttreja has been instrumental in transforming Aesthetics Group into a trusted name in property consultation.

Sushant Muttreja began his journey in real estate in 1998 with a vision to provide transparent, reliable, and customer-centric property services. His deep understanding of the market dynamics and commitment to ethical business practices has helped thousands of clients find their dream properties.

Under his leadership, Aesthetics Group has successfully completed over 5000 transactions across residential, commercial, and industrial segments, establishing itself as a leading real estate consultancy in Greater Noida, Noida Extension, and Yamuna Expressway regions.
